A Proclamation

concerning the small labours, noises & follies of one G. Bazuin
❖   ❦   ❖

Oyez, oyez! Oyez! Let it be known, unto all who encounter this parchment through whatever channel — RSS, search-engine, or mere happenstance — that the following tools, projects, noises and domestic follies are hereby set down, for the record.

This document doth not constitute a brand. It establisheth no funnel. No thought-leadership is herein offered, for it is the firm conviction of the undersigned that everything worth saying hath already been generated, elsewhere, by some larger language model.

I.
On Nerdy Terminal. Hereby established: a terminal-manager, keyboard-first, for macOS. Its proprietor refuseth to move his hand thirty centimetres unto the mouse. Mouse: banished. Sessions: instant. Keyboard: supreme.
II.
On the Kubernetes Estate. Let it be noted that the proprietor hath raised up his own control-plane of several clusters — wildly excessive for a one-person operation. When questioned, he whispereth only “GitOps” & turneth away.
III.
On the MCP Gateway. Be it known: a bridge hath been built between the AI-familiars and the greater world — Slack, Jira, databases, Grafana, Tailscale. It is the thing that maketh the other things to talk.
IV.
On Pixel Tanks. A multiplayer combat-game, in deliberate low resolution. Built for the joy of destroying one's friends. The pixels are intentionally retro. Friendships: tested.
V.
On TBD. A forthcoming productivity-engine. Captures. Projects. Tasks. No bells. No whistles. Release date: when it is done.
VI.
On the Home. A Home Assistant installation, held together by Zigbee and stubbornness. The WAF (domestic acceptance factor) sitteth at historic lows. Only the smart lamps have pleased the household. Two small heirs are in residence — they care for the lamps, not the clusters.
VII.
Of Noise & Sawdust. The proprietor beateth the drums in MAZU — an instrumental stoner ensemble, in which no one enquireth of lyrics. He playeth guitar badly, yet with zeal. A basement workshop accumulateth sawdust & ambition in roughly equal measure.
G. Bazuin
