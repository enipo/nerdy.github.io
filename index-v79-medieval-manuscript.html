Codex Nerdius

Beinge a true accounte of ye workes of one Bazuin, of Nederlande
N

OW hearken, gentle reader, & lend thine eye unto this codex, wherein are enumerated the small labours of a solitary maker — set down neither for glory, nor for commerce, nor for the teaching of thought-leadership.

Here are listed his engines, his noises, and his domestic follies; his kingdom of YAML; his half-finished benches; and the two small heirs who care for none of it, save the smart lamps.

— Writ this very day, upon the workbench at nerdy.nl.

Caput Primum

Of His Engines & Contrivances

Nerdy Terminal
Cathedra Clavium — the chair of keys

A terminal-manager most obstinate, forged for macOS. Its maker refuseth to move his hand thirty centimetres unto the mouse, and thus liveth by the keyboard alone.

Regnum Kubernetum
The kingdom of clusters

He hath raised up his own control-plane, with five clusters — a few too many, sayeth the chronicler. When asked wherefore, he whispereth only “GitOps” & returneth to his terminal.

MCP Gateway
Pons inter-machinarum

A bridge between the AI-familiars and the greater world: Slack, Jira, databases, Grafana, Tailscale. The thing that maketh the other things to speak one with another.

Pixel Tanks
Bellum pixellorum

A game of war in pixels most base, wherein friends destroy one another at low resolution. The art is intentionally retro — not, sayeth he, from any want of skill.

TBD
Opus in progressu

A productivity-engine: captures, projects, tasks. Neither bells nor whistles. Only that which must be done. Shipping date: shortly.

Domus Sapiens
Of the smart home

A Home Assistant held together by Zigbee & stubbornness. The co-proprietor calleth it a waste of weekends. He calleth it “the future of living.” The lamps alone findeth favour with the household.

Caput Secundum

Of Noise, Sawdust & Heirs

Tympanum & Chorda
Drums & guitar

He beateth the drums in an ensemble called MAZU — a band instrumental and stoned, wherein no man enquireth concerning the lyrics.

The guitar he playeth badly, yet with zeal. The neighbours — it is recorded — have opinions.

Officina Subterranea
The basement workshop

A workshop of wood in his cellar: at present, mostly sawdust and ambition. Every tool purchased is filed as “investment.”

Filii Duo
Of his heirs

Two small souls in residence. They careth nothing for Kubernetes, yet they careth mightily for the smart lamps — the sole chamber of the house wherein his works have found glad reception.
