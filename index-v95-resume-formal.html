— Abstract —

A short monograph documenting the professional output of one self-classified nerd: terminals that respect the keyboard, tanks rendered at low resolution, clusters that mostly stay up, and a modest collection of bridges between language models and the rest of the world. Years of experience: many. Yaks shaved: a similar number.

§ I.

Profile

\label{sec:profile}

Builder by inclination, infrastructure engineer by trade. Operates comfortably across the stack — from kernel-adjacent plumbing (Kubernetes, Terraform, Postgres) up to the user-visible surfaces (TypeScript, terminals, small games)1. Recent work has concentrated on agentic systems: the tools, gateways and protocols that allow language models to act on real infrastructure without breaking it.

Prefers small, sharp tools to large frameworks; long-lived projects to one-off scripts; and reproducible builds to demo magic. Will happily justify a YAML refactor with the same gravity normally reserved for refactoring a parser2.

side-note A The author admits to occasional Lisp sympathies. These are managed.
side-note B “Production” is a state of mind — usually somebody else’s.

§ IV.

Selected Projects

\label{sec:proj}
[01]
Nerdy Terminal — keyboard-first session manager (macOS)
TypeScript · Tauri
A multi-session terminal that treats the keyboard as the primary interface, the mouse as a polite fallback, and tabs as a problem already solved by tmux. Optimised for parallel work across clusters.
[02]
Pixel Tanks — top-down low-res multiplayer
TypeScript · Canvas · WebSockets
A small game in the lineage of the arcade-by-night, garage-by-day tradition. Pixel art, deterministic netcode, four directions of destruction, and surprisingly stubborn lag compensation.
[03]
Kubernetes / GitOps Estate — multi-cluster, production & lab
Terraform · Argo · Helm
A reproducible cluster topology with strict infrastructure-as-code and continuous reconciliation. Survives certificate rotation, node-pool churn and the occasional act of nature.
[04]
MCP Gateway — bridge: LLM ↔ Slack · Jira · Postgres · Grafana · Tailscale
Go · MCP · OAuth
A typed, audited gateway that exposes internal systems to language models through a single trust boundary, rather than around it. Designed to be boring to operate and hard to misuse.
[05]
Home Assistant Configuration — smart-home automation, in YAML, regrettably
YAML · Zigbee · MQTT
A long-running automation graph: light, climate, presence, and the careful avoidance of any switch that would inconvenience a non-technical household member.
[06]
TBD — a productivity application, currently codenamed
in progress
Captures, projects, tasks. Built around the observation that most productivity tools optimise for the wrong verb. More in the footnotes5.
