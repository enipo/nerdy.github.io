— Epistle from the workbench at nerdy.nl —
N

othing much to see here, save for a workbench at which one maker — G. Bazuin by name — buildeth small engines, strikes drums, accumulateth sawdust, and putteth, against his better judgement, the Y in Nerdy.

He automateth with YAML, whispereth GitOps at night, and keepeth a Home Assistant that is held together by Zigbee and obstinacy. His WAF — that domestic acceptance factor — resteth at historic lows. He remaineth, nevertheless, cheerful.

This letter is not a brand. It is not a funnel. It is neither a blog nor a thought-leadership platform. Everything worth saying hath already been generated by some LLM elsewhere. This is, modestly, a workbench.

Caput I
❦   ·   ❦   ·   ❦
Of engines, bridges, and battles
Nerdy Terminal A keyboard-first terminal-manager for macOS. The maker will not move his hand thirty centimetres unto the mouse. Instant sessions. No clicking. The way terminals should always have worked.
Kubernetes Estate A private control-plane, several clusters over-engineered for one lone operator. When asked wherefore, he stareth into the distance & whispereth GitOps.
MCP Gateway A bridge between the AI-familiars & all the other things: Slack, Jira, Grafana, databases, Tailscale. The thing that maketh the other things to speak one with another.
Pixel Tanks Top-down combat in blessed low resolution. Built for the joy of destroying friends. The pixels, saith he, are intentionally retro.
TBD A modest productivity-engine. Captures. Projects. Tasks. No bells. No whistles. Only that which must be done.
Home Assistant A smart house stitched from YAML. The smart lights are the sole chamber in which his labours findeth approval.
Caput II

Of noise, wood, and offspring
Drums — Mazu He beateth the drum-kit in an instrumental stoner band. In this music, no one enquireth concerning the lyrics.
Guitar Played badly, but with zeal. The neighbours have, it is recorded, opinions.
Basement Workshop A woodworking endeavour. Current state: mostly sawdust and ambition. Every tool acquired is filed as “investment.”
Two Small Heirs They care not at all for the clusters. They care mightily for the smart lamps. Audit ongoing.
