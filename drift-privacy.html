Drift privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · Applies to the Drift app for Android (app.drift.sleep) · Developer: NERDY

In short: Drift collects no personal data, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the Google Play version does not use the internet at all. Everything you make in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Drift stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your mixtapes and layers (which sounds, volumes, fades and schedules you chose);
  • your settings, such as the sleep timer;
  • a simple log of your listening sessions, used for the stats shown inside the app.

None of this is sent to NERDY or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Permissions, and why

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): to list the sound files and podcast episodes on your phone so you can add them to a mixtape. Drift reads the files to play them; it does not copy or upload them.
  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while a mixtape plays.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep audio playing while your screen is off, and to fade out at the end of the sleep timer.

Starter sounds

If you tap "Add starter sounds", Drift copies eight sounds that ship inside the app (rain, ocean, noise and so on) to the Music/Drift folder on your phone. These were generated by NERDY and need no download.

Backups

"Export mixtapes" writes a file to a place you choose; "Import" reads a file you choose. Nothing happens unless you start it. Because the app allows Android's system backup, Android may also include the app's private data in your Google account backup, depending on your phone's backup settings. That is handled by Android and Google, not by Drift; see Google's privacy policy for details.

Third parties

Drift contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no social login and no other third-party service that receives your data. The Google Play version of Drift does not request internet permission.

Children

Drift is a general-audience app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can remove everything Drift stored at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Files in Music/Drift are yours and stay until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@nerdy.nl.
