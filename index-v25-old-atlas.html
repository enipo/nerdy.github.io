Terminus MajorChief city & keyboard capital

Where nothing moves without a key-press.

A prosperous and somewhat stubborn city. Multiple terminal sessions throng its harbour; none of them use a mouse. Its tavern-keeper famously asked, “why move my hand thirty centimeters when I could press two keys?” and was elected mayor the following year.

Lat 52° N · Long 5° E · Nerdia, eastern shore
✹

Clusters IslesAn archipelago of computations

Here be GitOps. Proceed with caution.

Five islands where one would suffice. Each governs itself by an ancient YAML scroll that must be re-copied daily. The ruling council, when pressed for a reason, merely whispered “GitOps” and returned to its terminal.

Lat 53° N · Long 8° E · reachable only by control-plane
⇆

Pontis PeninsulaThe MCP Bridgehead

Where tongues meet and trade context.

A narrow peninsula which the inhabitants have fashioned into a great bridge. Slack, Jira, Grafana, Postgres, and Tailscale merchants all dock here. A single gatekeeper — the MCP — lets none pass without proper credentials and a well-formed schema.

Lat 51° N · Long 10° E · a narrow place of broad tongues
■

Pixel IslesIslands of low resolution

Square islands, square sorrows, sharp edges.

A crude archipelago whose coasts are made entirely of right angles. Small tanks roam the streets, firing upon each other with enthusiasm. Historians argue whether the pixel-art is retro by choice or by the limits of the draughtsman; the distinction, locally, means nothing.

Lat 50° N · Long 14° E · 16 colours, sometimes fewer
☮

Mazu BayA cove of drum-thunder

No lyrics. None requested.

A curved bay where an instrumental-stoner vessel named Mazu is most often moored. The drums are heard for some leagues inland; the neighbours have registered their opinions with local authority, who filed them neatly and returned to their drink.

Lat 50° N · Long 7° E · 60–80 BPM, seasonally
☎

Domus AutomatusA dwelling held by zigbee

WAF dangerously low; smart lights beloved.

A private holding where every lamp, door, and curtain is bound by invisible threads of radio. Three children reside within, who care nothing for clusters but everything for the lights. The lady of the house is unconvinced, but tolerates the arrangement.

Lat 52° N · Long 5° E · 600 lines of YAML
