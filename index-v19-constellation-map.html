Obs.: homelab

A map of small constellations, each a cluster of tools, habits and weekend projects orbiting the same house. Click a star to follow its name; hover a constellation for its lines to light up. Magnitudes are approximate; opinions are not.

Observer's note

No blog. No thought leadership. Only measurements, sketched in pen, over coffee. If it could have been a tweet, it was never recorded here.

Instrumentation

A laptop, a self-hosted Kubernetes cluster, a smart home held together by YAML and stubbornness, three children who remain unimpressed.

Method

Triangulate with curiosity, confirm with failure, annotate the result. Everything worth saying has been said by an LLM — the stars remain the author.

Motto

Putting the Y in Nerdy — as in: Why, dear god… Why!? Repeat nightly until the sky makes sense.
