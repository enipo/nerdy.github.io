Wing A — Tools & Implements

Cat. No. 2024.001
NERDY
working b. 1985 · Netherlands
Nerdy Terminal
2024–ongoing
macOS, Swift, stubborn keyboard shortcuts, zero mouse

A keyboard‑first terminal manager. The artist refuses to move his hand thirty centimetres to a trackpad, and instead has engineered a universe in which nine sessions switch with two keystrokes. The work raises urgent questions about efficiency, obsession, and whether “efficiency” is ever the real motive.

Gift of the artist, in perpetuity.

Cat. No. 2023.042
NERDY
working b. 1985 · Netherlands
Several Kubernetes Clusters, Home
2023–present
Mini‑PCs, k3s, Tailscale, ArgoCD, quiet desperation

A private control plane, wildly excessive for a household of five. Critics have described the installation as “over‑engineered,” to which the artist has responded only by whispering the word GitOps and returning to his terminal.

On long‑term loan from the basement.

Cat. No. 2025.011
NERDY
working b. 1985 · Netherlands
MCP Gateway (One Bridge to Rule Them All)
2025
Model Context Protocol, Slack, Jira, Postgres, Grafana, Tailscale

An ambitious work connecting AI agents to essentially everything else. The artist describes it flatly as “the thing that makes the other things talk to each other.” We find the modesty disingenuous.

Acquired through the General Acquisitions Fund.

Wing B — Diversions & Rituals

🎮
Cat. No. 2022.007
NERDY & FRIENDS
active 2000s–
Pixel Tanks
2022
HTML5 Canvas, WebSockets, deliberately low resolution

Top‑down multiplayer tank combat. Built for the joy of destroying friends in blocky, sub‑HD fashion. The pixel art is intentionally retro; no one has confirmed this is not a post‑hoc rationalisation of limited artistic ability.

Anonymous gift, in memory of a cancelled Saturday.

Cat. No. 2019.003
MAZU
active since 2019
Instrumental Stoner (ongoing study)
2019–
Drum kit, fuzz pedals, no lyrics, plenty of patience

The artist drums in Mazu, an instrumental stoner band. He also plays guitar — badly, but enthusiastically. The work engages themes of volume, repetition, and the opinions of neighbours.

Part of the Permanent Noise Collection.
🛠
Cat. No. 2025.022
NERDY
working b. 1985
Basement Workshop (in progress)
2024–
Plywood, sawdust, aspiration, every tool marked “investment”

A woodworking workshop slowly accreting beneath the family home. Current state: mostly shavings and ambition. The artist maintains that every power tool purchase is “an investment in the hobby,” a claim his accountant has privately questioned.

Funded in part by weekend energy.

Cat. No. 2021.099
NERDY, with spouse (coll.)
ongoing, acrimoniously
Home Assistant, Domestic (WAF Study)
2021–
YAML, Zigbee, Node‑RED, one very patient partner

A study of the Wife Acceptance Factor — a metric defined by the artist, rejected by his partner. The installation operates on what the artist calls a “shoestring budget” and what his wife calls “a waste of perfectly good weekends.” The smart lights, crucially, still work.

Mixed‑media installation; visitors may not touch.
