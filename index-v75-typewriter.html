A Brief Inventory,
Typed on a Tuesday

by G. Bazuin · for nerdy.nl

This is the first page of a very short book. It is being typed, not rendered. Pressing the RED key on the ribbon shifter is how emphasis happens around here; there is no bold, there is only leaning on the key harder.

The manuscript, in full, is as follows.

Chapter One · The Tools
  1. § 01Nerdy Terminal. A keyboard-first terminal manager for macOS. Nine sessions, no mouse, no regrets. Tab, tab, tab — as the author prefers it.
  2. § 02MCP Gateway. A bridge between AI agents and the rest of one's messy professional life: Slack, Jira, Grafana, Tailscale, and four three too many databases.
  3. § 03Pixel Tanks. Top-down multiplayer tank combat, rendered deliberately small, built for the joy of losing to friends.
  4. § 04TBD. A productivity application with no bells, no whistles, and no name yet, which the author finds appropriate.
  5. § 05Kubernetes. Five clusters. One engineer. The word “GitOps” is offered by way of explanation.
  6. § 06Home Assistant. A smart home held together by Zigbee and stubbornness. WAF at record low; the lights, at least, obey.
Chapter Two · The Author

The author plays drums in MAZU, an instrumental stoner band. The author also plays guitar, badly but enthusiastically; the neighbours have been informed, as a courtesy. The author has two children. They remain indifferent to his Kubernetes clusters and enthusiastic about the smart lights — the only only use case with a WAF above zero.

The author is also building a basement woodworking shop. Current state: mostly sawdust, mostly ambition. Every tool purchase is justified as an “investment in the hobby”; every hour in the shop is justified as “not looking at a screen”.

Chapter Three · Editorial

There is no blog here. There is no thought leadership, no content strategy, no newsletter, no course. Everything worth saying has already been generated by a large language model somewhere. This is just a workbench, and one types at it on Tuesdays.
