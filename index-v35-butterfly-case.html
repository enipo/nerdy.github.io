Curator's note

G. Bazuin collects the improbable. These specimens owe their existence less to necessity than to the stubborn belief that a thing worth doing is worth over-engineering. The cabinet is built of Dutch oak and weekend hours.

Field conditions

All specimens trapped with a homebrew net. Pins and labels hand-lettered. Preserved under a bell-jar of Nederland-time and instrumental stoner rock. Visitors welcome; small humans must be accompanied by an adult Kubernetes cluster.
