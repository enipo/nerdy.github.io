Tabulus nerdicus —
The Nerdy Terminal

Habitat
macOS deciduous forests
Behaviour
keyboard-dominant, mouse-averse
Range
cosmopolitan, preferring dim rooms

A diurnal species with pronounced aversion to pointing devices. Multiple sessions may be observed in a single individual, switched between by rapid percussion of the finger-tips. A typical call, recorded in the field, runs: “why would I move my hand thirty centimeters when I could press two keys?” Researchers report no satisfactory answer has yet been given.

Clusterum excessivum —
The Over-Engineered Cluster

Habitat
private datacentres & home closets
Behaviour
self-replicating, occasionally unreachable
Range
n = 5, where 1 would suffice

Notable for its baroque organisation. The organism operates its own control plane, consisting of far more clusters than any reasonable observer would deem necessary. When asked for a reason, the operator has been recorded staring into the middle distance, whispering a single word — “GitOps” — before returning to its burrow.

Pontifex multiplex —
The MCP Gateway

Habitat
interstices between APIs
Behaviour
polyglot, context-carrying
Symbiosis

A generalist bridging species. P. multiplex forms stable mutualisms with a surprising number of otherwise isolated organisms, carrying context between them as a pollinator carries grains. The specimen’s nomenclatural author describes it, with characteristic understatement, as “the thing that makes the other things talk to each other.”

Tankus octoseedicus —
The Pixel Tank

Habitat
LAN parties, basements, Tuesdays
Resolution
intentionally low
Packs

A small, angular predator with a penchant for open ground and sudden flanking manoeuvres. Its distinctive blocky plumage is often attributed, by the untrained, to the draughtsman’s limited ability; specialists agree, however, that the pixel-art is intentional retro, and that what appears to be low resolution is in fact the species’ preferred aesthetic.

† Known to sever friendships in bloom cycles of ~45 seconds.

Mazu instrumentalis —
The Instrumental Stoner Drummer

Habitat
rehearsal rooms, stages, vans
Call
60–80 BPM, bass-dominant
Lyrics
none (diagnostic)

A persistent vocal-suppressing species: though capable of sound at volume, M. instrumentalis produces no lyrics whatsoever. Its presence is most easily confirmed by the distinct thrum that reaches the neighbours’ homes. Said neighbours have been observed filing formal opinions; said opinions, so far, have had no measurable effect on the species’ continued performance.

† Sister species: Chorda enthusiasta (the enthusiastic-but-bad guitarist).

Domus automatum —
The Home of Automations

Habitat
a dwelling, three kids, zigbee
WAF
sub-zero (diagnostic)
Approved by
smart lights; toddlers

A cohabitant species, forming complex mutualisms with lamps, blinds and other household organisms. Its yaml-secreting glands produce upward of 600 lines per season. The Wife Acceptance Factor associated with its activity remains historically low; however, the smart lights — reportedly approved by the three resident children — are the single known use-case with a WAF above zero.

Subterra pulverifera —
The Basement Workshop

Habitat
basements, preferably low-ceilinged
Substrate
sawdust, ambition
Tools
“investments” (always)

A hypogeal species of recent emergence. S. pulverifera is characterised by constant low-frequency dust production, an ever-growing arsenal of tooling, and a deep and abiding belief that each new purchase qualifies, somehow, as an investment rather than a hobby. Its natural environment consists of plans drawn on napkins and projects begun over long weekends.
