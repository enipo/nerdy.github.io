How to use this guide

Each plate below describes a species of nerdy activity observed in the wild at domus-enipi. Note the call, the plumage, and the likely habitat.

  • C common — daily sightings
  • U uncommon — weekly
  • R rare — seasonal

Observer's notes

Most specimens are found in the keyboard layer of a Mac laptop. The rest are audible from the basement, usually as drum fills or as the whirring of small cooling fans.

tip — do not move the mouse. the subject will vanish.

common · year-round
