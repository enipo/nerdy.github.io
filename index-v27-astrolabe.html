·I·
De Artifice— concerning the maker

A maker of things nobody asked for. Automates the domestic with Home Assistant, provisions three Kubernetes firmaments for a household of five, and maintains a codex of terminals arranged in the shape of cascading tabs. The Wife Acceptance Factor is said to orbit near zero, but holds.

Three children, one basement workshop, one drumkit. Of these, only the smart lights are granted full citizenship.

·II·
Nerdy Terminal— ☾

A keyboard-first terminal manager for the Macintosh. Many sessions, one hand. The mouse remains in its drawer.

“Why move a hand thirty centimetres,” writes the maker, “when I might press two keys?”

·III·
MCP Gateway— ☿

A bridge between the court of the Agents and the older kingdoms: Slack, Jira, the Databases, Grafana, Tailscale. One gate. Many callers.

The thing that maketh the other things speak unto each other.

·IV·
Kubernetes— ♄

A private control plane of a few clusters too many. When asked why, the maker stared into middle distance and whispered GitOps. Wildly over-engineered for a household of one operator.

·V·
Pixel Tanks— ♂

Top-down tank combat rendered in deliberate low resolution. A game built for the destroying of friends, economically, with pixels. The art is retro by choice, not by limit.

·VI·
Mazu

Instrumental stoner. The kind of music where nobody asketh after the lyrics.

·VII·
Basement

Workshop in progress: sawdust, ambition, and a steady trickle of tool purchases each classified as investment.

·VIII·
Dad × 3

Three small citizens, entirely unconvinced by the clusters, wholly won by the lights.
