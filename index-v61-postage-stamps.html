About the Issue

This pane commemorates a small but persistent workshop located somewhere in the Netherlands. Each stamp is a project; each denomination, a confession.

Commonly found in mint condition, rarely cancelled — the issuer prefers his artefacts unpostmarked, though hovering the cursor produces an obliging cancellation.

— printed by hand in a basement, certified nerdy

Grading

MNH · mint never hinged. Production running. Feedback welcomed but not strictly necessary.

Provenance

One-person operation. No agency, no committee. Content strategy: none.

Errata

Denominations approximate. All values in “hours spent instead of sleeping.”
